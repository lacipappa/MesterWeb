COSTACARE – PROPERTY MANAGEMENT ONE-PAGE HERO

Folder: property-management-hero/
Suggested URL: https://lnkr.es/property-management-hero/
Languages: English, Spanish, Hungarian.

This is a compact, single-page HERO layout modelled on restaurante-hero:
- compact sticky header and EN / ES / HU language selector
- one main hero image with a short property-care introduction and contact links
- four concise service cards
- one compact service-area line
- contact strip with telephone, email and WhatsApp links
- footer and fixed mobile contact call-to-action

The original CostaCare service scope is retained:
- Property checks
- Cleaning and preparation
- Key holding and handovers
- Maintenance coordination

The original listed service areas are retained: Torrevieja, Orihuela Costa, Guardamar, Ciudad Quesada, Santa Pola and Alicante.

All three language JSON files are loaded dynamically by script.js. The phone number and email address are fictional demo details and must be replaced before publishing. Photographs are illustrative Unsplash images; see image-sources.html.
